# Dotnot Service Notice

Dotnot is currently a personal development fork. No public hosted service,
subscription offer, or deployment-specific terms of service have been
configured.

Running the source locally does not create an account or subscription with a
separate hosted operator. Any future deployment must identify its operator and
publish terms that match its actual service, support, billing, and data-handling
arrangements before offering access to others.

This notice does not establish subscription prices, refunds, service guarantees,
or a governing jurisdiction for a future Dotnot service. See the
[privacy notice](./privacy.md) for the current deployment status.
